Units & weighting

Agglomeration screening

A score (0–1) combines evidence that an object is itself a cluster (concavity, irregularity beyond its elongation, boundary complexity, relative size, elongation) with capped context evidence (contact with neighbours, watershed splitting, crowding). Classes above “questionable” need at least two independent signals. This is morphological suspicion only — SEM projections cannot prove physical agglomeration.

Resolution heuristic

Analysis heuristic, not a physical law: diameters spanning few pixels are dominated by digitisation and threshold placement.

Statistical tests

Particle-level tests assume independent observations. Particles from the same SEM field or sample are not independent experimental replicates; prefer image- or sample-level comparisons for inference.
